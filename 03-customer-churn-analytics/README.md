# 📊 Project 03: Customer Churn Analysis & Retention Strategy

[![Domain](https://img.shields.io/badge/Domain-Business%20Analytics-blue.svg)]()
[![Status](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Planned-lightgrey.svg)]()

Exploratory Data Analysis and predictive modeling to identify at-risk customers, uncover churn drivers, and recommend data-driven retention strategies for subscription businesses.

---

## 📌 Problem Overview
* **Type:** Business Analytics & Binary Classification
* **Target:** `Churn` (Yes / No)
* **Key Business Questions:**
  - What customer segments exhibit the highest churn probability?
  - How do contract types, tenure, payment methods, and technical support affect lifetime value (LTV)?
  - How can businesses optimize proactive intervention campaigns to maximize ROI?

---

## 🛠️ Planned Methodology
1. **Cohort & Funnel Analysis:** Customer lifecycle mapping and retention curves.
2. **Interactive Visualization:** Seaborn & Plotly dashboards highlighting high-churn customer personas.
3. **Classification & Lift Analysis:** ROC-AUC evaluation, Cost-Benefit Matrix analysis to balance false positives vs customer retention gains.
